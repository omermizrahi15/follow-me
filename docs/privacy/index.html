Follow Me

Privacy Policy

Last updated 19 August 2026

Follow Me lets a publisher share photos and videos with people who follow them on WhatsApp. This policy explains what the app collects, who it is sent to, how long it is kept, and how to get it deleted. It covers both publishers (people who post) and followers (people who receive posts on WhatsApp).

Who is responsible

Follow Me is an independent app operated by Omer Mizrahi, who is the data controller for the personal data described here. Questions, requests, and complaints: urishiber@gmail.com.

What the app collects

If you are a publisher

  • Your phone number. Used to sign you in — you receive a one-time code by SMS. It is also the identity your posts are attributed to.
  • Your profile. Display name, optional bio, and optional profile photo. Because followers can open your posts on the web, your display name, bio, and profile photo are publicly readable to anyone holding a link to your posts.
  • Photos and videos you post, along with their capture date, the number of items in the post, and — when the media carries GPS metadata — its coordinates and the place name derived from them.
  • Candidate photos. If you turn on automatic posting, the app uploads private copies of recent photos from your library so a post can be prepared while the app is closed. These copies are not shown to anyone; they exist so the app can choose what to suggest. See Photos in more detail below.
  • Your settings — posting schedule, how far back to look for photos, how many photos per post, category preferences, and whether posts need your approval.
  • Your follower list — the WhatsApp numbers of people who subscribed to you, and whether each subscription is active.
  • Device push token, so the app can notify you that a post is ready or was sent.

If you are a follower

  • Your WhatsApp phone number, which you provide by opening a publisher's invitation link or by messaging the app's WhatsApp number. It is stored against the publisher you chose to follow.
  • Delivery records — for each message sent to you, the message identifier, your number, the delivery status reported by WhatsApp (queued, sent, delivered, read, failed), and any error code. These exist so failed deliveries can be retried and so numbers that no longer work stop being messaged.
  • Messages you send to the app's WhatsApp number are processed to recognise commands such as STOP. Replies intended for a publisher are handled by WhatsApp between you and that publisher; the app does not read your conversation with them.

Diagnostics

The app sends crash and error reports so faults can be found and fixed. A report contains the error, the operation that failed, and technical details about the device and app version. It is not used to build a profile of you and is not used for advertising.

Photos in more detail

Photos are the most sensitive thing this app touches, so the handling is set out explicitly.

  • Reading your library. The app reads recent photos from your device library only after you grant photo access, and only within the time window you configure.
  • Cloud copies. Photos and videos — both the ones you post and the private candidate copies used to prepare posts — are uploaded to Cloudinary, a media hosting provider, and are served from there.
  • Automatic deletion. Candidate copies that fall outside your configured window are deleted automatically, with a seven-day grace period so a post awaiting your approval does not break.
  • Deleting everything, now. Settings → Privacy → Remove my photos from the cloud deletes every private copy the app holds and switches uploading off, so nothing is re-uploaded. Photos on your phone and posts you have already sent are not affected.
  • Choosing what to suggest. To suggest which photos are worth posting, the app sends photos to Google's Gemini API for classification. Google states that data sent to the paid Gemini API is not used to improve its models. Only photos in your selected window are sent, and only while automatic posting is on.
  • Location. If a photo carries GPS coordinates, the app stores those coordinates alongside the photo and sends them to BigDataCloud to turn them into a place name ("Lisbon, Portugal"), which appears in the post and in notifications. Photos without GPS metadata produce no location. The app does not track your device's live location.
  • Posted media is reachable by link. A post is delivered to followers as a WhatsApp message containing a link. That link is unguessable, but it is not password-protected — anyone your follower forwards it to can open the post. Treat a post as shared with everyone your followers might show it to.

Contacts

If you allow it, the app reads your device's address book so your followers can appear under the names you know them by instead of bare phone numbers. This matching happens entirely on your device. Your contacts are never uploaded, never stored on our servers, and never shared. Declining contact access only means followers are shown as phone numbers.

People who appear in photos

Photos often contain people other than the publisher. The publisher decides what to post and is responsible for having the right to share it — see the Terms of Service. We do not run face recognition and do not attempt to identify people in photos.

If a photo of you has been shared through Follow Me and you want it removed, email urishiber@gmail.com with the link to the post. We will remove the media from our storage. We cannot retrieve copies already delivered to followers' phones through WhatsApp.

International transfers

The providers above operate globally, so your data may be processed outside your country, including in the United States. Where data leaves the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, transfers rely on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses or an equivalent safeguard offered by the provider.

How long data is kept

  • Candidate photo copies — until they age out of your window (plus seven days), or until you delete them from Settings.
  • Posts and their media — until you delete the post. Deleted posts sit in the app's trash so you can restore them, and are removed from storage when the trash is emptied.
  • Follower records — until the follower unsubscribes or the publisher removes them. An unsubscribe is recorded so we do not message that number again.
  • Delivery logs — kept while needed to retry failures and diagnose delivery problems.
  • Account data — until you ask for the account to be deleted.
  • Crash reports — retained on Sentry's default schedule (90 days).

Your rights

Wherever you live, you can ask us to show you the data we hold about you, correct it, or delete it. Email urishiber@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days. We do not charge for this and will not treat you differently for asking.

If you are in the EU or UK (GDPR)

You have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you, and receive a copy of it in a portable format;
  • have inaccurate data corrected;
  • have your data erased;
  • restrict or object to processing;
  • withdraw consent at any time — for example by turning off photo upload, revoking photo or contacts access in iOS Settings, or replying STOP on WhatsApp. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that already happened;
  • complain to your local data protection authority.

We rely on consent for reading your photo library, uploading candidate copies, and reading contacts; on performance of a contract for running your account and delivering posts to your followers; and on legitimate interests for security, fraud prevention, and diagnosing crashes.

If you are in California (CCPA/CPRA)

You have the right to know what personal information is collected and who it is disclosed to, to delete it, to correct it, and to opt out of its sale or sharing. In the twelve months before the date above, we have not sold or shared personal information as those terms are defined by the CCPA, and we do not use it for cross-context behavioural advertising. The categories we collect are identifiers (phone number), photographic and audiovisual information, geolocation from photo metadata, and device diagnostics — all described above, all collected for the purposes described above. You may exercise these rights at the email address above, and may use an authorised agent to do so.

Deleting your account

Email urishiber@gmail.com from the phone number's associated address, or include the phone number you signed up with. We delete your account, profile, posts, uploaded media, settings, and follower list. Deletion is permanent. Messages already delivered to followers over WhatsApp remain on their devices and cannot be recalled.

Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) and stored with providers that encrypt data at rest. Database access is restricted so that a signed-in publisher can reach only their own rows. No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children

Follow Me is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, email us and we will delete it.
